eBay Raw Data · loading live data…
Every raw single matched to a card and first seen in the last 24 hours, newest first, with the buy-gate verdict for that listing. Listings with fewer than 2 photos are left out (you need the front and the back); suspect listings are hidden but reviewable. A listing priced under 40% or over 300% of the card's PriceCharting price is treated as a wrong match (verdict "mismatch") and left out of the statistics. "LEAD" marks a listing on a card eBay has too few sales to judge, priced from PriceCharting instead: a listing to look at, not a hit.
