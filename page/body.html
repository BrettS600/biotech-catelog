Favorites · the rows you starred, from every tab · kept by the collector, so the same stars show on every device
A star is saved on the collector the moment you click it (the page shows it at once and the next live file confirms it), so your favorites follow you from the laptop to the phone. Each view shows the starred rows through the columns of the tab they came from, with that tab's column choices; the Raw Data view also holds the auctions and hits you starred, and the listings starred on the Scam screen, in Mismatches, in the phone alerts and in the Review screen. "All" shows every starred card once, through the eBay Catalog's columns, with a note of where it was starred; cards eBay has nothing on yet show PriceCharting's price and dashes. The collector keeps following a starred listing to its outcome and never prunes it. Up to 400 favorites.
