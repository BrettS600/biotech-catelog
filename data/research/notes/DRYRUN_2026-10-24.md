# Dry run of the v2 rules on today's data (2026-10-24) — before the tab was rebuilt

`scripts/dryrun_today.py` ran the research's rules on the committed data/ folder (PriceCharting through this month, the
eBay catalog snapshot of 2026-10-23) exactly as the rebuilt tab computes them, to catch what the research's averages
could not show. Outputs `results/DRYRUN_2026-10-24_by_chance.csv` (the 60 best cards by chance at a 3-month hold — the
buy list), `DRYRUN_2026-10-24_candidates.csv` (the 60 deepest below their 12-month mean — the raw ranking),
`DRYRUN_2026-10-24_plans.csv`.

**What it said.** Market up (reg3 +1.7 %/mo, reg1 +2.2 %): the gate is green. 2,045 band cards with a rank, eBay supply and
a 3-month chance; 69 with a credible floor ≥ 20 % under the sold median; **25 clear a 50 % chance at 3 months**, 60 at 6
months — every one a card whose credible floor sits 15–24 % under a sold median with 5+ sales behind it (Armored Mewtwo
SM228 88 %, Umbreon & Darkrai GX SM241 84 %, Sabrina's Gengar 84 %, Misty's Gyarados 76 %, M Charizard EX #69 and #101
76 %, Gyarados EX, Mew XY110, Dialga V …). One copy each: ~$2,900 for an expected +$511 over 3 months.

**The surprises** (the point of the exercise):
* The top of the raw ranking is junk: cards whose PriceCharting print crashed on a single odd sale (Darkrai & Cresselia
  LEGEND at $40 against an $88 average, 2 eBay sales), promos with one $255 listing and no sales, cards with 0–2 eBay
  sales in 30 days. The tab's economics already score them 0–35 % (the buy is the eBay floor, far above the print), but a
  list sorted by rank would show them first.
* The old allocation (20 % of the capital per card, capped only by the eBay supply) bought 11 copies of Sabrina's Gengar
  and 23 of M Charizard EX #101 at $10,000 — never what the research tested (one copy per card) and not what eBay would
  absorb.
* The first version of the script let non-credible asks (under 75 % of the sold median — the collector's own rule) cap the
  sell level; corrected to credible asks only, which is what the page does.

**Changes made to the build because of it** (on top of FINDINGS): one copy per card by default (an input, 1–5); a card
needs at least one comparable eBay sale in the collector's 30-day window to be bought (an input, on by default); the
list is shown in buy order (chance, then rank), not rank order.
