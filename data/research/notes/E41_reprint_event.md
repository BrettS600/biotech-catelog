# E41 — reprint event study: Celebrations' Classic Collection (backlog B07)

Written 2026-10-26 (round 2). Script: `e41_reprint_event.py`. Data: the monthly panel.

**Question.** Trade press says "post-reprint panic selling is a buy". Celebrations (revealed June 2021, released
8 October 2021) reprinted 25 famous cards as the Classic Collection. What did the originals do relative to the rest of
their own sets?

**Method.** Originals found in the catalog: 18 plain / unlimited versions with prices on both dates (Base Set Charizard,
Blastoise, Venusaur, Imposter Professor Oak; Team Rocket Dark Gyarados, Here Comes Team Rocket!; Gym Challenge Rocket's
Zapdos; Neo Genesis Cleffa; Neo Revelation Shining Magikarp; Team Magma's Groudon; Rocket's Admin.; Mew ex; Gardevoir ex δ;
Umbreon ★; Claydol; Luxray GL LV.X; Garchomp C LV.X; Donphan; Reshiram; Zekrom; Mewtwo-EX; Xerneas-EX; M Rayquaza-EX;
Tapu Lele-GX — 24 ids, 18 with full data) and 5 of their 1st Editions. Abnormal log change = the card's log price
change minus the median log change of the other $5+ cards of its set, from May 2021 (before the reveal).

| to | plain originals: mean abnormal | median | share beating their set | 1st Editions: mean abnormal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021-06 (reveal) | +13.6% | +1.4% | 50% | −10.3% |
| 2021-08 | +26.4% | +14.2% | 61% | +15.0% |
| 2021-10 (release) | +18.5% | +7.5% | 67% | +9.1% |
| 2022-01 | +10.1% | +4.9% | 56% | −15.3% |
| 2022-04 | +18.5% | +4.6% | 67% | −6.6% |
| 2022-10 | +2.9% | −3.6% | 44% | −16.6% |
| 2023-04 | −20.2% | −24.7% | 33% | −27.3% |

**Reading.** The announcement was an attention event: the originals beat their sets by ~15–25% into the release, then
gave it all back and more over the following 18 months. There was no post-reprint dip to buy — if anything, the
originals were a sale into the announcement. One event, n = 18, during the 2021 mania; descriptive only.

**What would extend it.** Dates of other reprint / special-set announcements (Pokémon 151's Gen-1 art, Prismatic
Evolutions' Eeveelutions, 30th-anniversary 2026 products) and the cards they echo; the same event-time table per event.
